# 09 — Reveal a Round

**What to build:** When the team is ready, any Participant reveals, and every Estimate appears at the same moment. Identical values are grouped together and the highest and lowest are highlighted, because the disagreement is the reason to talk.

Nothing reveals on its own, and nobody has a special right to do it — a session must never stall waiting for one particular person to be at their keyboard.

**Blocked by:** 08 — Open a Round and cast a hidden Estimate

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Any Participant can reveal; there is no host and no permission check
- [ ] Nothing reveals automatically
- [ ] Revealing is possible before everyone has voted, so one absent teammate cannot block the team
- [ ] Every Estimate becomes visible at the same instant, with no window in which someone could adjust after seeing a number
- [ ] A revealed Round refuses new and changed Estimates
- [ ] Revealing an already-revealed Round is an error
- [ ] Identical values are grouped rather than listed one per Participant
- [ ] The highest and lowest Estimates are visually distinguished
- [ ] No average or median appears anywhere on the screen
- [ ] The Reveal reads as one moment across the table; ticket 15 refines the choreography but the integrity rule above is settled here
