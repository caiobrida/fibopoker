# 10 — Final Estimate and the next Round

**What to build:** After the discussion the team records the number they agreed on, then opens the next Round and keeps going. This is what closes the ceremony's loop and turns a Room into a record of what the session actually decided.

The Final Estimate comes out of people talking, so it is typed in — never derived from the Estimates on screen.

**Blocked by:** 09 — Reveal a Round

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A Final Estimate can be recorded on a revealed Round
- [ ] The Final Estimate is the only thing writable after a Reveal; Estimates stay immutable
- [ ] A Round can be revealed and moved on from without a Final Estimate ever being set
- [ ] The next Round can be opened, with no limit on how many Rounds a Room holds
- [ ] Finished Rounds are listed with their titles and Final Estimates
- [ ] The Final Estimate is entered by a person and never computed from the Estimates
