# 11 — Export the session

**What to build:** One action copies the session as a plain list of task and points, ready to paste into the team's board. Without it, somebody transcribes fifteen lines by hand at the end of every planning — which is the last place a tool should hand work back to a person.

What reaches the board is the Final Estimates: the numbers the team committed to, not the individual Estimates and not anything averaged.

**Blocked by:** 08 — Final Estimate and the next Round

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] One action copies the whole session to the clipboard
- [ ] The export carries Final Estimates, never individual Estimates and never a computed figure
- [ ] Rounds that never received a Final Estimate are represented unambiguously rather than silently omitted
- [ ] Rounds with no title are represented unambiguously
- [ ] Pasting into a plain-text destination produces readable output
